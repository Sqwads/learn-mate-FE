# LearnMate User Guide

Welcome to LearnMate! This guide will help you understand how to navigate and use the platform based on your role: **Student**, **Teacher**, or **School Administrator**.

---

## 1. Getting Started (Login & Sign Up)

- **Login**: All users (Admins, Teachers, and Students) can log in to LearnMate using their registered email and password on the home page.
- **Sign Up**: If your school is new to LearnMate, a School Administrator can create an account by clicking the "Sign up as school administrator" link on the login page.
- **Feedback**: You can share your experience with LearnMate by clicking the "Share your experience and help us improve LearnMate!" link on the login page.

---

## 2. School Administrator Role

The School Administrator is responsible for managing the school's account, provisioning users, and organizing classes.

### What Admins Can Do:
* **Analytics Dashboard**: Access an overview of the school's statistics, including the total number of students, teachers, active users, and classes.
* **Manage Users**:
  * Add students and teachers to the platform.
  * Use the **Bulk Upload** feature to add multiple users at once, saving time.
* **Manage Classes**:
  * Create new classes and update existing ones.
  * Assign teachers to specific classes.
  * Enroll students into their respective classes.

---

## 3. Teacher Role

Teachers use LearnMate to manage their classrooms, distribute assignments, take attendance, and track student progress.

### What Teachers Can Do:
* **Teacher Dashboard**: View an overview of the classes you teach and your total number of students.
* **Class Management**: View your assigned classes and the roster of students within each class.
* **Assignments & Assessments**:
  * **Create Assignments**: Set up new tasks for students to complete.
  * **Edit Assignments**: Modify assignment details and deadlines.
  * **Manage MCQs (Multiple Choice Questions)**: Create, upload, and view multiple-choice quizzes for students.
* **Grading & Submissions**:
  * View submissions turned in by students.
  * Review, grade, and provide feedback on student assignments.
* **Attendance**: Take and manage attendance records for your classes.

---

## 4. Student Role

Students use LearnMate to keep track of their learning journey, submit assignments, and view their attendance and grades.

### What Students Can Do:
* **Student Dashboard**: Get a quick overview of your enrolled classes, average grades, and pending vs. completed assignments.
* **Class Information**: Access details about the classes you are currently enrolled in.
* **Assignments**:
  * **Pending Assignments**: View tasks you haven't completed yet.
  * **Submitting Work**: Complete and upload file assignments.
  * **Taking MCQs**: Answer multiple-choice questions natively within the platform.
  * **Completed Assignments**: Review the tasks you've already turned in.
* **Attendance Tracking**: View your personal attendance records for each class to ensure you are meeting requirements.
